## English

Draft pending the lawyer's review.

Daily Dose can compare your score with other people who finished the same daily game. There is no account. This browser gets a random anonymous ID. Daily Dose does not ask for your name or email.

What is sent: the five category scores, the day, and the time of the request, with that anonymous ID.

Why: to show your score beside the average of everyone else who finished that day.

What is kept: the scores stay. They are kept as a combined daily total and under the anonymous ID. Rate-limit records and request metadata are deleted after 7 days.

Who it is for: people 13 and older in the United States.
